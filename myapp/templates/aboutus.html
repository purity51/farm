About the company

Lenzis Agro LTD is a professionally managed agribusiness enterprise focused exclusively on large-scale agricultural production and market distribution. The company specialises in high-demand crops and livestock, supplying consistent, high-quality products to both local and international markets.

Strategically located in Nthia, along Wote-Makindu Road, the company operates on approximately 50 hectares of land, enabling scalable, efficient, and commercially viable agricultural operations.

Meet the Founder

Emmanuel Mbelenzi

"Yellow Trail" — Founder & Visionary

Emmanuel Mbelenzi is the founder of Mbel Capital Holdings Limited, a company built on the pillars of discipline, resilience and mental awareness. Established in 2020 during the Covid-19 pandemic, Mbel Capital Holdings has grown into a diversified investment group, among them Lenzis Agro Ltd — focused on agriculture, crop and animal production.

A self-driven entrepreneur, Emmanuel operates the largest watermelon farm in Kajiado County — over 16 hectares producing more than 1,600 metric tonnes per year.
